How it works

  1. Book online. Pick your destination, group size, and times. Pay by card — that's it.
  2. We meet you at the pier. Our representative is at our meeting point, a 10 minute walk from the ship, holding a sign with your name. The board reads Visit Cozumel Shuttles & Tours — the same family company, so look for that and your name. See the walk from your pier →
  3. We come back at your time. Your driver returns to the same spot you were dropped at.
Your driver drops you off and comes back at the time you chose — that return time is locked in when you book. He isn't parked outside all day, which is what keeps the price flat instead of hourly.
