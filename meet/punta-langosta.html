Meeting point

Punta Langosta

Downtown San Miguel. This is the terminal in the middle of town, with the pedestrian bridge over the main road.

Look for

The white lighthouse

It stands between Hooters and Starbucks, right on the seafront. Our representative is underneath it, holding a sign with your name on it.

No signal when you step off? Most phones have no data in Cozumel until you buy a plan. Take a screenshot of this page while you still have the ship's wifi — the lighthouse, Hooters and Starbucks are all you need to find us.

What you'll see

Step by step

  1. Walk off the ship and straight out through the terminal shops.
  2. Cross the road on the pedestrian bridge.
  3. Take the stairs down at the far end of the bridge.
  4. Walk toward the white lighthouse — between Hooters and Starbucks.
  5. Our representative is waiting there with a sign showing your name.

If you need us

Message us on WhatsApp and tell us where you're standing. We answer in English, and someone is on the pier the whole time your ship is in port.
