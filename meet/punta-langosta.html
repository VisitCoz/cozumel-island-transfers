Getting there

  1. Walk off the ship and straight out through the terminal shops.
  2. Cross the road on the pedestrian bridge.
  3. Take the stairs down at the far end.
  4. Walk toward the white lighthouse — between Hooters and Starbucks.
  5. We are waiting there with a sign showing your name.
No signal when you step off? Most phones have no data in Cozumel until you buy a plan. Screenshot this page while you still have the ship's wifi — the lighthouse, Hooters and Starbucks are all you need to find us.
