Getting there · about 10 minutes

Aim to be with us 15 minutes before your pickup time — so leave the ship about 25 minutes before.

The whole route is flat and paved, with a sidewalk the entire way. No steps and no hills.

1Walk out of the terminal. There is only one way out. Turn right and start looking for the Royal Village Shopping Center sign.Start
2You will pass a tall Royal Village sign — the one with the shop names down it. Keep walking.2 min
3At the corner you will see the second Royal Village sign. The crossing is just ahead.4 min
4Now you can see the crossing. The bus in this photo is parked at our meeting point — that is where you are going.5 min
5Cross the street here. The meeting point is where the bus is parked.6 min
6We are here. Your van or bus is parked here, and our representative is waiting with a sign — reading Visit Cozumel Shuttles & Tours — holding your name.You’re here
