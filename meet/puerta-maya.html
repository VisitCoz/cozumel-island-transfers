Getting there · about 10 minutes

Aim to be with us 15 minutes before your pickup time — so leave the ship about 25 minutes before.

The whole route is flat and paved, with a sidewalk the entire way. No steps and no hills.

1Turn left and do not cross the street. Stay on the ocean side.Start
2You will pass vendors, taxis and people selling. Keep going until you see the gas station.1 min
3Once you see the Pemex gas station, you are going the right way. Keep on the sidewalk.2 min
4You will see Royal Village Mall.4 min
5That is where you cross the street.5 min
6Once you cross, we are here — waiting in your van or bus, with a sign holding your name. The board reads Visit Cozumel Shuttles & Tours — that is us.You’re here
