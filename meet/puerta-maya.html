Puerta Maya Cruise Terminal

Two minutes from the pier

Out of the terminal, turn left, and stay on the sidewalk by the ocean — about two minutes. Same spot for both southern piers.

The exact spot

Drag the map, or switch to Street View, to see the corner before you arrive. It needs an internet connection — do it on the ship's wifi.

Getting there

1Walk out of the terminal and turn left.
2Do not cross the street. Stay on the sidewalk on the ocean side and keep walking.
3You will pass vendors, taxi drivers, rental stands and people selling tours. This is normal. Nobody there is your driver. Say no thank you and keep going.
4About two minutes along, we are waiting for you with a sign showing your name.
No signal when you step off? Most phones have no data in Cozumel until you buy a plan. Screenshot this page while you still have the ship's wifi — the map below is the exact spot.
