How it works
  1. Book online. Pick your destination, group size, and times. Pay by card — that's it.
  2. We meet you at the pier. Our representative is at our meeting point right outside the terminal, a few steps from all three piers, holding a sign with your name.
  3. We come back at your time. Your driver returns to the same spot you were dropped at.
Your driver drops you off and comes back at the time you chose — that return time is locked in when you book. He isn't parked outside all day, which is what keeps the price flat instead of hourly. We follow the port authority's arrival feed, and one of our team checks it again before every pickup — so a late arrival never costs you the transfer.

Who we are

Cozumel Island Transfers is a licensed private ground-transfer operator based in San Miguel de Cozumel, Quintana Roo. We've been picking up cruise guests and cruise guests on this island since 2015.

Every vehicle is private — never shared. A/C, bottled water, daily inspection. We pick up at all three cruise terminals: Puerta Maya, the International Pier and Punta Langosta.
