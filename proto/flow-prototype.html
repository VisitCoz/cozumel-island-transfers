What happens, step by step
  1. You walk off the ship at your own pace. No rush — we track your ship.
  2. A few steps outside the terminal, our representative is holding a sign with your name on it.
  3. You ride in a private, air-conditioned vehicle — just your group, bottled water, English-speaking driver.
  4. You're dropped at and you enjoy your day.
  5. At the time you chose, your driver is waiting at the same spot to bring you back.
  6. You're back at the pier at the time you picked. We recommend leaving at least 90 minutes before your ship's all-aboard time.
