How it works
  1. Book online. Pick your destination, group size, and times. Pay by card — that's it.
  2. We meet you at the pier. Our representative is at our meeting point right outside the terminal, a few steps from all three piers, holding a sign with your name.
  3. We come back at your time. Your driver returns to the same spot you were dropped at.
The vehicle doesn't wait with you all day — that's what keeps the price flat instead of hourly. We monitor your ship in real time, so a late arrival never costs you the transfer.

Who we are

Cozumel Island Transfers is a licensed private ground-transfer operator based in San Miguel de Cozumel, Quintana Roo. We've been picking up cruise guests and cruise guests on this island since 2015.

Every vehicle is private — never shared. A/C, bottled water, daily inspection. We pick up at all three cruise terminals: Puerta Maya, the International Pier and Punta Langosta. When you message us on WhatsApp, you're texting a young, fun local company.

  1. You walk off the ship at your own pace. No rush — we track your ship.

  4. You're dropped at and you enjoy your day.
  5. At the time you chose, your driver is waiting at the same spot to bring you back.
  6. You're back at the pier at the time you picked. We recommend leaving at least 90 minutes before your ship's all-aboard time.
