Puerta Maya — three walkthrough layouts

Same six steps, same words, three ways of showing them. Nothing on the live site has changed. The grey frames are where your Street View screenshots go — each one is labelled with the shot it holds.

Layout 1
The Filmstrip
Scroll down. One big photo, one instruction, six times. No interaction to learn and nothing hidden — the safest of the three for a nervous 70-year-old. Longest page.

Puerta Maya Cruise Terminal

Across from Royal Village Mall

Turn left out of the terminal and stay on the ocean side. Two minutes, no crossing.

Getting there

1Walk out of the terminal and turn left.
south-2.jpgThe sidewalk on the ocean side
2Do not cross the street. Stay on the sidewalk on the ocean side.
south-3.jpgThe stretch with vendors and taxis
3You will pass vendors, taxis and people selling tours. This is normal. None of them is your driver.

4You will see a Pemex petrol station. That means you are going the right way — keep going.

Layout 2
The Trail
The grammar of walking directions — a line down the left connecting numbered dots, a thumbnail beside each. She can see the whole route at once and judge how far it is before setting off. Photos get small, and Street View detail is the first thing lost.

Layout 3
The Swipe
One step fills the phone; swipe or tap the arrows for the next. The best-looking of the three and the most Tesla. Also the riskiest — a guest who never swipes sees step one and nothing else, which is why the arrows are real buttons and not just a gesture.
