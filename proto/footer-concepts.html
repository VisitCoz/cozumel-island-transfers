Three footers

Built from research into how Apple, Tesla, Stripe, Linear, Booking, Airbnb, Viator and GetYourGuide actually do this. All three agreed on the same four things:

  • One type size, one weight. Yours ships seven sizes and uses weight 800 six times. Not one of the benchmark sites exceeds weight 510 or uses more than one size.
  • Cut the eight destination links. A footer nav list is an SEO habit for multi-page sites. You're one page — it earns nothing and repeats the photo grid. GetYourGuide, with thousands of destinations, carries none.
  • Cut TripAdvisor. Zero of Booking, Airbnb, Viator, GetYourGuide or Marriott put a review score in the footer. Viator is owned by TripAdvisor and still doesn't.
  • The phone gets smaller, not bigger. Emphasis moves from size to colour — and it gains the dialling instruction, which is what actually makes an international number usable.
