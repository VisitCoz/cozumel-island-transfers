Where does my cruise ship dock in Cozumel?

Cozumel has three cruise terminals: Puerta Maya, the International Pier and Punta Langosta. Here is where each of the 45 ships that called at least 10 times in the last 12 months has docked, from the port authority's records.

We say “usually” only when a ship used one terminal on at least 70% of at least 3 calls in the last 12 months. The 6 ships that split their calls say “it varies”, with the split.

Before you plan around this: The port sets each ship's berth day by day. Our booking bar looks up her exact date, and we confirm your terminal by email the day before.

Where these numbers come from

The Quintana Roo port authority's published schedule, updated weekly. “The last 12 months” means 25 September 2025 to 24 September 2026; cancelled calls are left out, and calls at anchor are never given a pier. Each guide also shows the ship's calls since 1 January 2024. Listed: every ship with at least 10 calls in the last 12 months.
